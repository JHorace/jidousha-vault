# Autonomy
**Fantasy**: characters live their own lives — the world the player
overrides, not a menu waiting for input. Most characters are sedentary
(home tile); the ones who move are notable.

**Loop**: the scorer — candidate actions weighed by traits, motivators,
regard, bonds/grudges, desperation. One decision function, two callers
([[asks]] injects a heavily-weighted candidate into this same scorer).
**Action vocabulary (seeded)**: seek work / work an industry slot /
join an open party / pursue or poach a petition / socialize / idle.
Desperation reshapes the weights (broke characters take worse work —
the labor market self-balances).

**Degrades to**: characters idle at home — the giri-rt S1 world.

**Open**: scorer cadence (per world-hour?) · action vocabulary growth
discipline (each new module adds candidate actions — declared in its
capsule) · gifts/theft/loans as later actions (the loan shark implies
loans exist in fiction).
