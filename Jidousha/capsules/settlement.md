# Settlement
**Fantasy**: a place worth prospering — but prosperity is **capacity,
not victory**: the settlement sizes the player's reach. Quests are the
progression; the settlement is what lets you get there.

**Loop**: the player's own task agenda (build the bridge, farm the
land) originates here. **Industries are standing job slots** the
autonomy scorer fills — autonomous employment, "passive" only from the
player's chair. Player levers are chunky: build an industry (treasury
sink), per-industry wage, and a **levy knob (drawer constant, default
0)** — expedition income stays the focus; passive income is something
the player upgrades into. MVP ships **one generic industry** so the
limp-floor exists.

**Post-MVP**: the **adventuring company** industry — institutionalizes
the player's early behavior, absorbing routine threats as the cast
grows (the phasing arc: the player automates themselves). Industries
can also be born from [[aspirations]] (the baker's dream becomes a
bakery).

**Degrades to**: a static town that neither grows nor gates; no
autonomous employment (needs then rest entirely on party work).

**Open**: what buildings do mechanically beyond job slots ·
construction costs/times (drawer) · settlement-level reputation
(attracts arrivals?).
