# Knowledge (experiment)
**Fantasy**: knowing your people is earned. A wandering band nobody
knows is genuinely unknown; a friend's hidden want is a quest hook.

**Loop**: lens parameters over true sim state. **v1 ships all-visible**
(decided 2026-08-29); this module is the layered-on experiment.

**Degrades to**: the identity lens - exactly v1.

**Variants** (fills for this slot, each independently testable):
- regard-unlock - traits/wants reveal as regard rises (leading)
- transitive - you know a little of what friends know; nobody's
  friend = nobody's known (live)
- rumor-monger - purchased edges: intelligence for wealth (live)
- world-fog - map-level discovery; oppressive-fog hard mode as a
  preset (parked)

**Open**: playtester budget rule applies - fresh eyes reserved for
this variant family; never burn a naive tester on the open build.
