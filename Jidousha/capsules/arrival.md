# Arrival
**Fantasy**: where characters come from. Newcomers who might settle,
and the notable movers — merchants, mercenary bands — whose mobility
is itself a signifier.

**Loop**: population as slow inflow; visitors as opportunity and
(with [[knowledge]]) uncertainty. **Generational specialization
candidate (recorded)**: arrival-pool quality/specialization keyed to
settlement development — mature industries attract gen-2 specialists.

**Degrades to**: a fixed cast.

**Open**: what attracts newcomers (capacity? settlement reputation?) ·
departure/leaving (the pariah spiral's exit) · the other
specialization mechanism (aptitude change through use — see
[[aspirations]] / [[trait-vocabulary]]).
