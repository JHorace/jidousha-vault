# Aspirations
**Fantasy**: dreams. The parked giri P3 long-term goals, mechanized:
**an aspiration is a petition-generator** — a character dreaming of
owning a shop emits a sequence of concrete petitions (milestones).
Fulfillment mutates the sheet and writes a title-mark.

**The baker dream (recorded)**: aspirations that pull *against* player
utility — your best adventurer wants to bake. Honoring it costs the
sword and pays an industry + title-mark + regard (the P1 retirement
template, matured; some industries come from dreams). Watch for
**emergent rivalry** — a character whose aspiration cuts against the
player is an opponent by the same rules; observe before designing
opponents.

**Career mechanics candidate (recorded)**: aptitude change through
repeated work — sheet mutation generalized to careers; one of the two
generational-specialization mechanisms.

**Degrades to**: short-term motivator petitions only.

**Open**: curdling rates + milestones as the mitigation (P1 finding
5) · legacy/heirs on retirement.
