# Threats
**Fantasy**: goblins exist. External pressure on the settlement.

**Loop**: the antagonist generator — pressures the settlement AND
**emits petitions into the same ledger** (the farmer wants the goblins
gone). **Two tiers (phasing arc)**: routine threats, absorbable by the
adventuring-company industry once it exists; **heroic threats**,
requiring manual parties of the most capable — escalation keyed to
settlement development, not a timer.

**Degrades to**: a peaceful world.

**Open**: do threats hold territory on the map · threat escalation
curve ownership (director?).
