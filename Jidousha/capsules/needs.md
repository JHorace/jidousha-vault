# Needs
**Fantasy**: everyone has to make a living. The ambient upkeep field —
constant pressure, engaged through chunky knobs, never per-person
attention.

**Loop**: needs are a **data-defined list with one v1 entry**: coin
upkeep per interval, burned from the character's wallet (the fiction
of food lives in display — "can't afford bread"). **Traits modulate
upkeep cost** (data on the trait row: lavish costs more, ascetic
less). Shortfall raises **desperation** (existing field; source line
rewritten) — the first rung of the **escalation pipe**: shortfall ->
desperation -> petition -> grudge -> autonomy consequences. **Upkeep
is earned, not paid**: party work (wages at dispatch) + autonomous
employment at [[settlement]] industries. No tax lever; stipend/charity
is a trait-flavored variant, not built.

**Principle — the settlement must limp without you**: autonomous
employment holds most characters at subsistence with the player idle;
attention buys the difference between limping and flourishing.

**Verify — economy sweeps**: ~200 idle-player seeds; median wallet and
treasury within named bands; nobody-starves-at-subsistence asserted
(the limp-floor as a test); a mutated wage constant must break a band.

**Degrades to**: no upkeep — wealth accumulates without pressure; the
field vanishes, petitions and asks still function.

**Open**: intervals and denominations (drawer) · food as a second
entry (triggered by a famine/siege design need, not before).
