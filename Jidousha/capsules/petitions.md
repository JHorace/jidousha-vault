# Petitions
**Fantasy**: people ask you for things, to your face, with a deadline.
"Get me 50 gold by next month so I can pay off a loan shark." The
petition ledger IS the quest board — content generated by the cast,
poachable by autonomous characters.

**Loop**: three sources, one ledger — motivator-generated, need-
shortfall-escalated, director-injected (the director speaks in
petitions). **Obligation = voicing**: a petition binds the player only
when delivered (instant audience in town; messenger travel otherwise —
obligation arrives by the channel orders leave by). Deadline is a
**cliff**; the consequence is declared on the card at voicing time
(one-source: the warning IS the consequence). Failure: regard hit +
the declared consequence fires (often a director event) + a
grudge-fact only on repeat/egregious. One active petition per
character. Credit: whoever satisfies it (player credit when the player
arranged it); incidental satisfaction = full credit when the condition
is objectively met. Character-offered rewards pay from the
petitioner's wallet — poor petitioners pay in regard.

**Degrades to**: a cast without demands; regard moves only through
asks and needs.

**Variants** (fills, not built): decline — honest no at discounted
cost (leading candidate) · promise — pauses the clock, breach is
worse · richer failure resolution (regard down with one character, up
with another) · expectation scaling.

**Open**: does the holder need to *know* to credit (parked at
[[knowledge-lens]]) · petition templates as data (shared with the
director).
