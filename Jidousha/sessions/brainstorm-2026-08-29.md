## Decisions (chronological)
- Core fantasy: indirect settlement-builder — rule by request.
- Aptitudes as a trait kind; no list cap yet (RTwP legibility decides;
  cap moved to [[attention]]).
- Asks travel (instant audience in town; messenger remote).
- Prosperity = capacity, not victory; quests are the progression.
- v1 all-visible; [[knowledge-lens]] seam from day one; [[knowledge]]
  is a layered-on experiment. Playtester budget: never burn a naive
  tester on the open build.
- Design-confidence scheme adopted pending review-in-use (process doc
  section 5b / 7).
- **Needs/petitions split**; "wants" retired as a term. Escalation
  pipe; three petition sources, one ledger; the director speaks in
  petitions. N1 gold-only · N2 desperation · N3 upkeep earned not
  paid (party wages + autonomous employment; no tax lever) · N4
  aggregates for the glance, faces for the action. W1-W7 per the
  petitions capsule. M1 mint/burn/conserve · M2 treasury-margin ·
  M3 petitioner wallets pay rewards · M4 levy knob default 0 ·
  M5 upkeep burn only, trait-modulated · M6 economy sweeps.
- **The settlement must limp without you** (design principle).
- **The phasing arc**: the player automates themselves — hand-cycled
  parties -> adventuring company absorbs routine threats -> heroic
  threats need manual parties; generational specialization; the
  baker dream.
- **MVP set (confirmed)**: foundation + autonomy + asks + petitions +
  needs + resolution + minimal settlement (one generic industry) +
  the minimal injector (3-4 canned petition-flavored templates).
  Parties, threats, arrival, aspirations, knowledge, full director:
  post-MVP.
