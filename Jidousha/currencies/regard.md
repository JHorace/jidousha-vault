# Regard
The master currency: **directed edges**, char->player and char->char.
Political capital — satisfied petitions raise it, failed voiced
petitions cut it, wage generosity moves it. Also an information
network: you know a little of what your friends know (see
[[knowledge]]). giri v2's regard edges are the played ancestor.

**Scoping (resolved 2026-08-29)**: the player is taxed only by
**voiced petitions** (obligation = voicing); ambient needs never drain
regard directly — they escalate through desperation into petitions.
Expectation scaling (loved-expect-more) retired to a petitions
variant. Drama pays in regard when wallets cannot pay in gold.
