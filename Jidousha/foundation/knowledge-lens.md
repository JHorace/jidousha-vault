# Player-knowledge lens (foundation obligation)
The sim runs on truth; everything the player sees passes through a
knowledge filter. **v1 lens = identity (all visible).** Every UI
surface reads through the lens from day one so the [[knowledge]]
experiments become lens parameters, not screen rework. One truth, one
lens, many settings.
